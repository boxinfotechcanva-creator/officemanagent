FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is NEET mandatory for MBBS abroad?

Yes, NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students seeking MBBS abroad as per NMC guidelines. However, the cutoff is much lower compared to Indian medical colleges.

What is the total budget for MBBS abroad?

The total budget ranges from ₹20-50 Lakh depending on the country and university. Countries like Bangladesh and Uzbekistan offer affordable options starting from ₹20 Lakh.

Is MBBS abroad valid in India?

Yes, MBBS degrees from NMC-approved foreign universities are valid in India. Students need to pass the FMGE/NEXT exam to practice medicine in India.

What documents are required for admission?

Key documents include: 10th & 12th mark sheets, NEET scorecard, passport, passport-size photographs, and a medical fitness certificate.

Which country is best for low-budget MBBS?

Bangladesh and Uzbekistan are the most affordable options for MBBS abroad, with total fees starting from ₹20 Lakh for the entire course duration.

Do universities provide hostel and Indian food?

Yes, most NMC-approved universities provide hostel accommodation and Indian mess facilities for international students.
